About Cricgoals

Who We Are

Cricgoals is an independent global sports news platform dedicated to cricket and football. We publish match reports and expert analysis for fans in every corner of the world — clearly written, quickly updated, and always free to read.

Cricgoals was founded by Sheryar Khan with a simple conviction: great sports journalism should be open to everyone, everywhere. What began as one fan's passion for the game has grown into a platform followed by readers in countries across the world.

What We Cover

Our coverage spans the two games the world loves most. In cricket: international series, Test matches, ODIs and T20 internationals, and the world's leading leagues — including the PSL, IPL, Big Bash League, CPL, ILT20, BPL and WPL — together with the ICC Cricket World Cup, the T20 World Cup, the Champions Trophy and The Ashes. In football: the FIFA World Cup, the Premier League, La Liga and the UEFA Champions League. Alongside the news, our match analysis keeps fans connected to every turning point of every game.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to make trustworthy, high-quality sports coverage available to every fan, free of charge and free of paywalls. We believe a fan in Karachi, London, Toronto or Sydney deserves the same fast, accurate and enjoyable reading experience — so we write in plain, clear language that puts the game first and respects the reader's time.

Our Editorial Standards

Accuracy comes first. Scores, fixtures and facts are checked against official sources before anything is published, and if we ever get something wrong, we correct it openly. We report fairly and without bias: facts are kept separate from opinion, and confirmed news is never mixed with rumour. Where a story rests on a source, we say so — our readers should always know what is established, and what is not yet confirmed.

Languages

Sport is a global language, and so is Cricgoals. Every part of the site — scores, headlines and articles — is available in three languages: English, Roman Urdu and Urdu, so that fans across South Asia, the worldwide South Asian diaspora, and readers everywhere else can follow the game in the language they are most comfortable with. Use the language switcher at the top of any page to change language at any time.
