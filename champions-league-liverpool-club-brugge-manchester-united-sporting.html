Champions League: Isak brace fires Liverpool past Club Brugge, Fernandes double not enough for Manchester United

Alexander Isak scored twice as Liverpool won 3-0 at Club Brugge, while Bruno Fernandes scored twice in three minutes but Manchester United were held 2-2 by Sporting in Lisbon.

By Sheryar Khan • 26 Nov 2026

BRUGGE / LISBON: Liverpool and Manchester United had very different Champions League nights on Wednesday. Liverpool won 3-0 at Club Brugge to move to 12 points from five league-phase games, while United drew 2-2 at Sporting after leading in the second half.

In Brugge, Alexander Isak gave Liverpool an early lead after goalkeeper Yann Sommer's attempted pass was intercepted by Bradley Barcola. Dominik Szoboszlai, captain for the night with Virgil van Dijk on the bench, pulled the ball back and Isak finished first time. Liverpool lost Victor Munoz to injury before half-time, but his replacement Rio Ngumoha later set up the second goal.

Florian Wirtz curled in Liverpool's second in the 79th minute, in off the left post from the edge of the box, and Isak added his second three minutes later, dinking the ball over Sommer. The brace took Isak to 10 goals for the season, and Wirtz has now scored in three successive games. Head coach Andoni Iraola said the 3-0 scoreline flattered his side, but it was Liverpool's fourth Champions League win of the season, with FC Porto at home next.

In Lisbon, United's night swung wildly. Goalkeeper Senne Lammens saved Luis Suarez's first penalty, then conceded a second spot-kick about a minute later by fouling Maxi Araujo, and Suarez put Sporting ahead in the 33rd minute. Amad Diallo had a goal disallowed before half-time for a foul by Youri Tielemans.

Bruno Fernandes, back at his former club, turned the match around after the break. He equalised from the penalty spot in the 48th minute, then put United in front two minutes later when his long-range effort bounced off a divot in the pitch and over goalkeeper Kaique. But Lammens spilled a high ball in the 80th minute and Suarez levelled for Sporting. United thought Kaique's own goal from a Fernandes corner had won it, only for VAR to rule it out for a foul by Patrick Dorgu, and Tielemans was sent off in stoppage time.

The draw denied Mauricio Pochettino his first win as United head coach, a week after taking charge and four days after the 4-0 defeat at Liverpool. United are 19th in the Champions League table and in the Premier League relegation zone, with Brentford next on Saturday. Pochettino said he was proud of his players' personality and character despite the frustration.

Sources: Liverpool FC, Sky Sports, Reuters.
