India vs Pakistan U19 Asia Cup Final: India win by 1 wicket off the last ball — Mohit Ulva 49*

Mohit Ulva hit a boundary off the final ball as India chased 338 to beat Pakistan by one wicket in the U19 Asia Cup final at Al Amerat — India's ninth title, and their first since 2021.

By Sheryar Khan • 14 Nov 2026

AL AMERAT, OMAN: India are Under-19 Asia Cup champions again — and they won it in the most dramatic way possible. Chasing 338 against defending champions Pakistan in the final at the Al Amerat Cricket Ground on Saturday, India were down to their last wicket and needed three runs off the final ball. Mohit Ulva, batting at No. 10, smashed leg-spinner Ahmed Hussain wide of long-off for a boundary, and India finished on 339 for 9 — a one-wicket win off the very last ball of the match, and India's ninth Under-19 Asia Cup title, their first since 2021.

Pakistan's 337: Usman 107, Qadir 80

Pakistan won the toss and chose to bat, and their openers gave them the start a final demands. Usman Khan and Hayam Khan put on 62 in 8.1 overs, and then Usman and Abdul Qadir took over — 135 runs in 18 overs. Usman reached his hundred in 106 balls and made 107 off 111, with 11 fours and three sixes, while Qadir smashed 80 off just 54 balls, with eight sixes and three fours, reaching his maiden Youth ODI fifty in 39 balls. Captain Farhan Yousaf added 60 off 63 balls, and Pakistan were 240 for 3 in 36 overs, on course for much more. But India's bowlers fought back in the last ten overs — Kavya Patel took 4 for 68 and Chigurupati Venkata 3 for 40 — and Pakistan finished on 337 for 9.

India 252-8 — then Mohit and Manal

India's chase began well. Captain Lakshya Raichandani (80 off 101) and Aaryavir Sehwag (58 off 61) added 95 for the first wicket, and India looked in control. Then the middle order collapsed, and by the 42nd over India were 252 for 8, with the trophy slipping away. That was when two unlikely heroes stepped in. Manal Chauhan (47 off 32) and Mohit Ulva put on 57 for the ninth wicket, dragging India back into the match. Manal fell with the score on 302, caught by Usman Khan off Niqab Shafiq, and India still needed 36 runs with only one wicket left.

Last ball, last wicket

Mohit found one more ally in last man Chigurupati Venkata (11 not out off 8), and the pair kept India alive. India needed eight runs off the last over, and then three off the last ball. Mohit went down the track to Ahmed Hussain and smashed the ball wide of long-off for four — and the chase was done. "It was a roller coaster," said captain Lakshya Raichandani afterwards. "We always had the belief."

Ninth title, first since 2021

The win is India's ninth Under-19 Asia Cup title, and their first since 2021. It also avenges their group-stage defeat to the same opponents — Pakistan beat India by three wickets on 8 November, with Hamza Zahoor scoring an unbeaten 100 — and it takes the trophy from the defending champions, who had beaten India by 191 runs in the previous edition's final. Aaryavir Sehwag was named Player of the Tournament.
