India vs New Zealand 1st Test live score: New Zealand 368 all out — Nicholls 175*, Phillips 118

New Zealand recovered from 70/5 to post 368 in the first innings on Day 2 of the first Test at Basin Reserve, Wellington, as Henry Nicholls finished 175 not out and Glenn Phillips made 118 — India have begun their reply in today's match.

By Sheryar Khan • 20 Nov 2026

WELLINGTON: New Zealand posted 368 in their first innings on Day 2 of the first Test against India at Basin Reserve, Wellington, recovering from 70/5 through a 216-run stand between Henry Nicholls and Glenn Phillips.

Nicholls finished 175 not out off 335 balls, his 12th Test hundred, while Phillips made 118 — his maiden Test century — before Akash Deep bowled him at 286-6. India had won the toss and chosen to bowl, and Jasprit Bumrah led the attack with 4-92, with Akash Deep taking 3-53 and Anshul Kamboj 2-69.

India have begun their reply in today's match: Yashasvi Jaiswal made 85 off 89 balls before falling to Matt Henry, and KL Rahul is 43 not out off 82 balls, with Devdutt Padikkal 4 not out off 3 balls. India are 135/1 after 28.3 overs, with 234 runs still needed. At tea, India were 16/0. Rishabh Pant is keeping wicket after clearing his fitness test ahead of this series.

The second Test of the two-match series starts on 27 November at Hagley Oval, Christchurch.

Sources: ESPNcricinfo, Sportradar, NDTV, News18, The Hindu.
