Liverpool 4-0 Man Utd: Pochettino's nightmare debut at Anfield

Mauricio Pochettino's first game as Manchester United head coach ended in a 4-0 defeat at Liverpool, with Leny Yoro sent off after eight minutes and the hosts two goals up inside 13 minutes.

By Sheryar Khan • 22 Nov 2026

LIVERPOOL: Liverpool beat Manchester United 4-0 at Anfield on Sunday, 22 November, handing Mauricio Pochettino a heavy defeat in his first game as United head coach.

The game turned inside eight minutes. A mistake by Matthijs de Ligt — making his first Premier League start in over a year — let Alexander Isak through on goal, and Leny Yoro's foul from behind brought a straight red card. Dominik Szoboszlai curled the resulting free kick into the bottom corner in the 10th minute, and Florian Wirtz doubled the lead three minutes later from Rio Ngumoha's cross. Pochettino sent on Harry Maguire for Bryan Mbeumo in the 22nd minute and switched to a back five, but Liverpool stayed in control.

Ngumoha made it 3-0 in the 66th minute after Bradley Barcola's run, and substitute Victor Munoz completed the scoring in the 82nd minute from Wirtz's pass. United captain Bruno Fernandes almost grabbed a late consolation, but Alisson Becker parried his stoppage-time effort.

The win lifts Liverpool to fourth in the Premier League, one point behind Brighton and Manchester City. United stay 19th, one point from safety, now nine league games without a win — their longest such run since 1972 — and with 34 goals conceded, the worst defence in Europe's top five leagues. Ngumoha, at 18 years and 85 days, became the youngest player to record a goal involvement in a Premier League game between these two sides. Wirtz, who scored once and set up another, was named man of the match.

Both sides are in Champions League action on Wednesday — Liverpool away at Club Brugge and United away at Sporting Lisbon. Liverpool then travel to Everton for the Merseyside derby, while Pochettino's first home game in charge comes against Brentford next Saturday.

Match stats: possession 64%-36%, shots 28-10, on target 11-4, corners 15-5, fouls 15-7.
