Man Utd sack Michael Carrick, Pochettino deal agreed — announcement expected today

Manchester United confirm Michael Carrick has left as head coach, and Mauricio Pochettino has agreed a two-and-a-half-year deal — club announcement expected on Friday.

By Sheryar Khan • 13 Nov 2026

Manchester United have confirmed that Michael Carrick has left his role as men's first-team head coach. The club announced the departure on Thursday, 12 Nov, with United 18th in the Premier League.

Pochettino deal agreed

Mauricio Pochettino is set to replace him: Fabrizio Romano reported on Wednesday night that the deal is done, with a green light from the US Federation, and David Ornstein of The Athletic reports Pochettino will sign a two-and-a-half-year contract until 2029. United are set to pay around £7.4m–£7.5m ($10m) to trigger the release clause in his USA contract.

Announcement expected, USA games first

A club announcement about Pochettino could come as soon as Friday, 13 Nov, but it has not happened yet — so the appointment itself is still "agreed", not yet officially announced by the club. Pochettino will stay with the USA for two CONCACAF Nations League games against Haiti, on Saturday 14 Nov and Tuesday 17 Nov, before travelling to Manchester. His first United match is expected to be against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday 22 Nov.

Sources: The Athletic, Fabrizio Romano, Malay Mail, The Times.
