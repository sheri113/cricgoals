New Zealand vs India 2nd Test Day 1: Latham, Blundell fifties

New Zealand reached 246 for 5 at stumps on Day 1 of the Christchurch Test against India, with fifties from Tom Latham and Tom Blundell after Akash Deep's two wickets.

By Sheryar Khan • 27 Nov 2026

CHRISTCHURCH: New Zealand closed Day 1 of the second Test against India on 246 for 5 at Hagley Oval on Friday, fighting back after Akash Deep's two wickets had cut them from 110 for 1 to 110 for 4.

India captain Shubman Gill won the toss and chose to bowl on a green, seaming pitch, but Tom Latham and Devon Conway added 77 for the first wicket. Debutant Gurnoor Brar trapped Conway lbw for 31 with the last ball before lunch to claim his first Test wicket, and Latham went on to 59 off 128 balls — his 32nd Test fifty after two two-ball ducks in Wellington.

Akash Deep then changed the day. He had Latham caught behind by Rishabh Pant, Anshul Kamboj dismissed Henry Nicholls for 16, and Akash Deep removed Rachin Ravindra for a duck as three wickets fell without a run being added. Akash Deep finished with 2 for 52, while Jasprit Bumrah went wicketless.

Daryl Mitchell's 31 steadied New Zealand alongside Tom Blundell in a 74-run stand before Washington Sundar had Mitchell caught at leg slip. Blundell, playing his 50th Test, reached an unbeaten 53 off 117 balls, and Glenn Phillips was 36 not out as the pair added an unbroken 62 to take the hosts to stumps in better shape.

The two-Test series is level at 0-0 after the draw in Wellington, so the winner in Christchurch takes the series. Play resumes on Saturday at 11:00 am local time (3:00 am PKT).

Sources: ESPNcricinfo, AFP, PTI.
