New Zealand vs India 2nd Test Day 2: India reeling after Blundell 89, Akash Deep five-for

India slid to 116 for 6 in reply to New Zealand's 356 on Day 2 of the Christchurch Test, despite Akash Deep's five-wicket haul and Tom Blundell's 89.

By Sheryar Khan • 28 Nov 2026

CHRISTCHURCH: New Zealand tightened their grip on the second Test against India on Saturday, bowling the visitors down to 116 for 6 in reply to 356 after Tom Blundell's 89 and Akash Deep's five-wicket haul had shaped the day at Hagley Oval.

New Zealand resumed on 246 for 5 and reached 356 all out in 116.3 overs. Blundell, playing his 50th Test, made 89 off 211 balls with 10 fours before falling 11 short of a century, caught by Akash Deep at fine leg off Gurnoor Brar. Glenn Phillips went early for 36, but Nathan Smith, Kyle Jamieson and Matt Henry all chipped in as the last two wickets added 38 runs.

Akash Deep finished with five wickets to become only the fifth Indian fast bowler to take a five-wicket haul in a Test innings in New Zealand. Brar and Anshul Kamboj took two wickets each, while Jasprit Bumrah went wicketless in 26 overs.

India's reply fell apart against the seamers. Matt Henry bowled Yashasvi Jaiswal for a six-ball duck in the first over, Kyle Jamieson removed Devdutt Padikkal for 21 and captain Shubman Gill for 17 to leave India 59 for 3 at tea, and Jacob Duffy then struck twice after the break — Rishabh Pant for 23 and KL Rahul for 25 — as India slid to 116 for 6, Washington Sundar falling for 10 to Nathan Smith with the last ball of the day to leave Dhruv Jurel unbeaten on 7 and India 240 behind.

The two-Test series is level at 0-0 after the draw in Wellington, so the winner in Christchurch takes the series. Play resumes on Sunday at 11:00 am local time (3:00 am PKT).

Sources: ANI, PTI, Times of India.
