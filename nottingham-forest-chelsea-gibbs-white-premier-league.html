Nottingham Forest vs Chelsea: Gibbs-White double sinks Chelsea in Premier League

Morgan Gibbs-White scored twice as Nottingham Forest beat Chelsea 3-2 at the City Ground, handing Xabi Alonso's side a third successive Premier League defeat.

By Sheryar Khan • 28 Nov 2026

NOTTINGHAM: Chelsea slumped to a third successive Premier League defeat on Friday as Morgan Gibbs-White scored twice in Nottingham Forest's 3-2 victory at a rainy City Ground.

Igor Jesus volleyed Forest in front inside five minutes, and Chelsea never recovered parity. The visitors did not manage a shot on target in the entire first half, and the second half started just as poorly when Wesley Fofana brought down Liam Delap and Gibbs-White converted the penalty in the 51st minute.

Substitute Emmanuel Emegha pulled a goal back three minutes later, but Gibbs-White restored Forest's two-goal cushion in the 72nd minute, clipping home Xaver Schlager's pass. Emegha headed in Pedro Neto's cross in the 89th minute to set up a nervy finish, yet Forest held on for a deserved three points — back-to-back home wins under Oliver Glasner after beating Manchester City last time out at the City Ground.

The defeat leaves Chelsea eighth and piles further pressure on Alonso, whose side have now lost three league games in a row.

Sources: Reuters, Sky Sports, AP.
