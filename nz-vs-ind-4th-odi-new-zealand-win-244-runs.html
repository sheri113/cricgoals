New Zealand vs India 4th ODI: New Zealand win by 244 runs — Will Young 125, Matt Henry 6-27

Will Young's 125 powers New Zealand to 333 at Bay Oval, then Matt Henry's 6-27 bowls India out for 89 — New Zealand win the 4th ODI by 244 runs and keep the series alive at 2-1.

By Sheryar Khan • 13 Nov 2026

New Zealand kept the ODI series alive with a huge 244-run win over India in the 4th ODI at Bay Oval, Mount Maunganui, on Friday, 13 Nov. India stand-in captain Shreyas Iyer won the toss and chose to bowl, but Will Young made a career-best 125 off 108 balls (14 fours, 4 sixes), Tim Seifert hit 58 off 48 and Finn Allen 41 off 30 as New Zealand posted 333 all out in 50 overs.

India collapse to 89

Axar Patel took 2-39, and Auqib Nabi and Gurnoor Brar took two wickets each; Kuldeep Yadav went for 0-73 in eight overs. India's chase collapsed at once: Rohit Sharma fell first ball and Virat Kohli on the fifth ball of the first over, both for ducks, and India lost three wickets before scoring a run — the first time that has happened in India's ODI history. Ruturaj Gaikwad fell for 5 and India were 9-4. Matt Henry took 6-27 as India were bowled out for just 89, with Ishan Kishan top-scoring on 27.

Series alive at 2-1

New Zealand win by 244 runs. India still lead the five-match series 2-1 after the 3rd ODI was abandoned for rain; the 5th and final ODI is on Sunday, 15 Nov, at Bay Oval, Mount Maunganui, starting 7:30 AM IST.

Sources: NZ Herald, The News Mill, Daily Pioneer / ANI.
