Pakistan lead Sri Lanka by 23 runs after Day 1 in Rawalpindi — Azan Awais 47 not out

Pakistan bowled Sri Lanka out for 98 and reached 121-3 at stumps on Day 1 of the series decider in Rawalpindi, with opener Azan Awais unbeaten on 47.

By Sheryar Khan • 17 Nov 2026

RAWALPINDI: Pakistan took control of the second and final Test against Sri Lanka on Tuesday, bowling the visitors out for 98 and reaching 121-3 at stumps — a first-innings lead of 23 runs with seven wickets in hand.

Pakistan won the toss and chose to bowl first, and the decision paid off at once. Sri Lanka slipped to 25-3 and then 36-5, with Razaullah striking twice to remove Lahiru Udara and first-Test centurion Dhananjaya de Silva. Only captain Kusal Mendis (32) and Sonal Dinusha (21) reached double figures for the visitors.

Mendis and Dinusha added 49 for the sixth wicket, but Mendis fell just before lunch with Sri Lanka on 85-6, and the last four wickets fell for 13 runs after the break. Mohammad Abbas took 3-14, Razaullah 3-21 and Aamer Jamal 3-24, with Saqib Khan taking the other wicket. Sri Lanka's 98 is the lowest innings total at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium, beating England's 112 from 2024.

Awais holds firm

Openers Azan Awais and Tayyab Arif put on 80 for the first wicket in reply. Tayyab made 46 before falling, and Mohammad Taha and captain Babar Azam both made 8 — but Awais stayed firm, finishing unbeaten on 47 from 96 balls, with six fours. Saqib Khan was 2 not out at the close, and the pair will resume on the second morning.

With the series still 0-0 after the drawn first Test, Pakistan are in a strong position to press for the win that would take the series. Play resumes on Wednesday at 9:30 AM PKT.
