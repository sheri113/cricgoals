Pakistan lead Sri Lanka by 153 runs after Day 2 in Rawalpindi — Ghazi Ghori 54

Pakistan were bowled out for 325 and reduced Sri Lanka to 74-3 in their second innings at stumps on Day 2 in Rawalpindi, with Ghazi Ghori top-scoring with 54.

By Sheryar Khan • 18 Nov 2026

RAWALPINDI: Pakistan tightened their grip on the second and final Test against Sri Lanka on Wednesday, posting 325 and then reducing the visitors to 74-3 in their second innings at stumps — a lead of 153 runs with seven wickets still to take.

Pakistan resumed on 121-3 and lost Azan Awais early for 47, but Ghazi Ghori held the lower order together with a composed 54, adding 56 for the ninth wicket with Razaullah (42). Nightwatchman Saqib Khan made 37, and Lahiru Kumara finished with 5-95 for Sri Lanka.

In reply, Sri Lanka lost three early wickets — Lahiru Udara for 7, Dinesh Chandimal for a duck and Kamindu Mendis for 16 — before Pasindu Sooriyabandara (18 not out) and Dhananjaya de Silva (24 not out) steadied the innings with an unbroken 35-run stand. Bad light ended play early for the second day running, with nearly 45 overs now lost across the two days.

With the series still 0-0 after the drawn first Test, Pakistan are in a strong position to press for the win that would take the series. Play resumes on Thursday at 9:30 AM PKT.
