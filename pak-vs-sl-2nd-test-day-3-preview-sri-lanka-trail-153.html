Pakistan vs Sri Lanka 2nd Test Day 3 preview: Sri Lanka trail by 153 with seven wickets left

Sri Lanka resume on 74-3 on Day 3 in Rawalpindi, still 153 runs behind Pakistan — Dhananjaya de Silva and Pasindu Sooriyabandara must build a big partnership to keep the series decider alive.

By Sheryar Khan • 19 Nov 2026

RAWALPINDI: Sri Lanka resume on 74-3 on Thursday (9:30 AM PKT) on Day 3 of the second Test against Pakistan, still 153 runs behind with seven wickets in hand — and needing 154 more runs just to make Pakistan bat again.

Pakistan tightened their grip on Wednesday, posting 325 and then reducing the visitors to 74-3 in their second innings. Ghazi Ghori top-scored with 54, his maiden Test fifty, while Lahiru Kumara took 5-95 — the first five-wicket haul by a Sri Lankan bowler in a Test innings at Rawalpindi. Sri Lanka had been bowled out for 98 in their first innings, the lowest total at this ground.

The key battle

Everything rests on the overnight pair. Pasindu Sooriyabandara (18 not out) and Dhananjaya de Silva (24 not out) have added 35 unbroken runs, and Dhananjaya — who made 167 in the first Test — is the wicket Pakistan want most. If the pair survives the first hour, Sri Lanka can start thinking about saving the match; if Pakistan strike early, an innings win is on the cards. Pakistan head coach Mike Hesson still described his side's batting as "scrappy", so the hosts will want a sharper day with the ball.

Weather

Thursday looks dry — partly cloudy, a high of 25°C and no rain expected — so a full day is possible on the weather. But bad light has already ended play early on both days, with nearly 45 overs lost, and sunset is just after 5pm, so another early finish is possible.
