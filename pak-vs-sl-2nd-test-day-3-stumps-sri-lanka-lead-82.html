Sri Lanka lead Pakistan by 82 runs after Day 3 in Rawalpindi — Milan Rathnayake 60 not out

Sri Lanka reached 309-9 at stumps on Day 3 in Rawalpindi, turning a 153-run deficit into an 82-run lead as Milan Rathnayake's unbeaten 60 kept Pakistan waiting.

By Sheryar Khan • 19 Nov 2026

RAWALPINDI: Sri Lanka fought their way out of a 153-run deficit on Thursday, reaching 309-9 at stumps on Day 3 of the second and final Test — an 82-run lead with one wicket left, after a day of determined batting at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium.

Sri Lanka resumed on 74-3 and lost both overnight batters early — Dhananjaya de Silva for 37 and Pasindu Sooriyabandara for 38 — before captain Kusal Mendis (53) and Sonal Dinusha (54) rebuilt with an 81-run stand. Mendis reached 5,000 Test runs during his innings, becoming the 12th Sri Lankan to the mark, before falling to Salman Ali Agha at 215-6.

Dinusha added 53 more with Milan Rathnayake, whose unbeaten 60 — his second Test fifty — carried Sri Lanka past 300 and into the lead. Aamer Jamal was Pakistan's most successful bowler with 3-67, while Mohammad Abbas took 2-54. Pakistan were a bowler short all day: Saqib Khan, who strained his left hamstring on Wednesday, could not bowl.

Pakistan need one more wicket before chasing what should still be a modest target, with the series 0-0 after the drawn first Test. Play resumes on Friday at 9:30 AM PKT.

Sources: Geo.tv, Dawn, ThePapare, The Express Tribune.
