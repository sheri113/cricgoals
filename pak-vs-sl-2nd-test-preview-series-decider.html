Pakistan vs Sri Lanka 2nd Test preview: series decider in Rawalpindi after drawn opener

Pakistan and Sri Lanka meet in the series decider at Rawalpindi on Tuesday — the first Test ended in a draw, the weather looks better this week, and the winner takes the two-match series.

By Sheryar Khan • 17 Nov 2026

RAWALPINDI: Pakistan and Sri Lanka meet in the second and final Test at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium on Tuesday (9:30 AM PKT), with the two-match series still to be decided after the first Test ended in a draw.

The opener, at the same ground, produced only three innings. Pakistan declared on 434-9, Sri Lanka replied with 446, and Pakistan finished on 284-8 — rain and bad light costing 117 overs out of 360 across the five days. Saud Shakeel was the difference for Pakistan with 93 and 97 — 190 runs in the match — while Dhananjaya de Silva (167) and Kusal Mendis (119) made Sri Lanka's runs, and debutant Saqib Khan took 7-130.

What is at stake

The winner takes the series and World Test Championship points. A draw would leave the series 0-0. In Tests overall, Pakistan lead Sri Lanka 23-17 across 60 matches, with 20 drawn — but the last two meetings between these teams in Rawalpindi were both weather-affected draws.

Team news — two calls still open

Sri Lanka are without Pathum Nissanka, ruled out injured, with Lahiru Udara added to the squad and expected to open. They may also bring in spinner Prabath Jayasuriya if the pitch looks drier than the one used for the first Test. For Pakistan, Ali Shabbir could make his debut — which would be the fifth Pakistan debut of the series. The playing XIs are confirmed only at the toss, around 9:00 AM PKT.

Pitch and weather

The Rawalpindi surface is expected to be good for batting early on, with the spinners finding more purchase than they did in the first Test as it wears. Winning the toss and batting first looks the good decision. The weather is much better than last week: sunny today, a high of 24°C and no rain expected all week — though with sunset just after 5pm, some early finishes due to bad light are still possible.
