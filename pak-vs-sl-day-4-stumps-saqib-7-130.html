Pakistan vs Sri Lanka 1st Test Day 4: Saqib Khan takes 7-130 on debut, Pakistan lead by 45

Debutant Saqib Khan's 7-130 bowls Sri Lanka out for 446 in Rawalpindi, then Pakistan reach 57-1 at stumps on Day 4, leading by 45 with one day left.

By Sheryar Khan • 13 Nov 2026

Pakistan fought back on Day 4 of the first Test in Rawalpindi on Thursday, 12 Nov. Sri Lanka resumed on 404-4, just 30 behind, but debutant fast bowler Saqib Khan struck in the first over, dismissing Dhananjaya de Silva for 167, and Sri Lanka added only 42 runs before losing their last six wickets for 446 — a lead of just 12.

Saqib takes seven on debut

Saqib finished with 7-130 in 34 overs on debut. Kusal Mendis made 119. Pakistan then reached 57-1 in their second innings at stumps, leading by 45, with Azan Awais 22 not out and Mohammad Taha 24 not out; Tayyab Arif fell for 11, lbw to Asitha Fernando.

Early finish, Day 5 ahead

Play ended early after tea because of bad light and rain — only 21.2 overs were possible in Pakistan's second innings. Day 5, the final day, starts today, Friday 13 Nov, at 9:30 AM PKT, but morning rain is forecast in Rawalpindi (98-100% chance 7-9 AM), so a draw looks the most likely result. The 2nd Test starts Tuesday 17 Nov, also in Rawalpindi.
