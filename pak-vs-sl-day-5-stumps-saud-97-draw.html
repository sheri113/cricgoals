Pakistan vs Sri Lanka 1st Test Day 5: Saud Shakeel 97 saves draw in Rawalpindi

Saud Shakeel's fighting 97 saves Pakistan on Day 5 in Rawalpindi — Pakistan finish 284-8, match drawn, series level ahead of the 2nd Test on 17 Nov.

By Sheryar Khan • 14 Nov 2026

Pakistan saved the first Test on the fifth and final day in Rawalpindi on Friday, 13 Nov, as the match ended in a draw. Resuming on 57-1, leading by 45, Pakistan lost two wickets in the first two overs — Azan Awais fell for 22 to the first ball of the day from Lahiru Kumara, and Mohammad Taha was caught behind for 24 off Asitha Fernando. Captain Babar Azam made 34 and Salman Agha 13, both falling to a leg-side trap off Milan Rathnayaka, leaving Pakistan on 125-5.

Saud's 97 holds the innings together

By lunch, Pakistan had reached 151-5, a lead of 139. Saud Shakeel then held the innings together with a fighting 97 off 210 balls (7 fours), adding 50 with Babar and 92 with Ghazi Ghori (35) for the sixth wicket, before Lahiru Kumara bowled him three short of a hundred. Pakistan finished on 284-8, a lead of 272, and the teams shook hands. Saud made 190 runs in the match (93 and 97).

Series level at 0-0

Rathnayaka was the best bowler with 3-47, Fernando took 2-35 and Kumara 2-65. Bad light and rain cost the match 117 overs out of 360 across the five days. The series is level at 0-0; the second and final Test starts on Tuesday, 17 Nov, also in Rawalpindi.

Sources: Dawn / AFP, SuperSport, News18.
