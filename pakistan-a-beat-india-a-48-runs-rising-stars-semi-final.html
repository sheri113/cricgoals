Pakistan A beat India A by 48 runs to reach Asia Cup Rising Stars semi-finals

Hasan Nawaz starred with bat and ball as Pakistan A beat India A by 48 runs in Doha to reach the Asia Cup Rising Stars semi-finals — Tilak Varma's hundred in vain.

By Sheryar Khan • 22 Nov 2026

DOHA: Pakistan A beat India A by 48 runs at the West End Park International Cricket Stadium in Doha on Sunday, 22 November, to reach the semi-finals of the ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026.

The defending champions, who won the toss and batted first, posted 313 all out in 49.2 overs. Hasan Nawaz top-scored with 79 off 82 balls, Maaz Sadaqat made 59 and Farhan Yousaf 56, with the openers putting on 66 and Hasan adding 65 with captain Abdul Samad. Harshit Rana, Prince Yadav, Suryansh Shedge and Vipraj Nigam took two wickets each for India A.

India A, chasing 314, were bowled out for 265 in 49.1 overs. Abhishek Sharma hit 70 off 42 balls, and captain Tilak Varma made 100 off 149 — his second consecutive century of the tournament — but he was the last man out, dismissed by Ahmed Daniyal on the very next ball after reaching his hundred in the 50th over. Shahbaz Ahmed's 56 helped Tilak add 112 for the seventh wicket from 129 for 6, but the final four wickets fell for 24 runs. Hasan Nawaz took 3 for 45 and Ali Raza 3 for 56, and Hasan was named player of the match for his 79 and three wickets.

The win, Pakistan A's second successive Group B victory after beating UAE by 61 runs in their opener, confirms their semi-final qualification with a game to spare. They top the group with four points and finish the group stage against Nepal on Tuesday, 24 November. India A face UAE the same day, with that result deciding the fourth semi-finalist; Sri Lanka A and Bangladesh A have already qualified from Group A.

The match also carried a toss controversy: according to Dawn, the match referee initially called tails incorrectly after Tilak flipped the coin on Samad's call of heads, before Samad pointed out the error and officials corrected the decision in Pakistan's favour. The two captains did not shake hands.

Sources: ESPNcricinfo, Dawn, The Nation, Qatar Tribune, Geo Super, Express Tribune, Cricbuzz.
