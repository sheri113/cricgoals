Pakistan beat Sri Lanka by 8 wickets to win Test series 1-0 in Rawalpindi

Pakistan chased down 90 in 13 overs on the fourth morning in Rawalpindi to beat Sri Lanka by eight wickets and seal the two-match series 1-0 — their first Test series win in two years.

By Sheryar Khan • 20 Nov 2026

RAWALPINDI: Pakistan beat Sri Lanka by eight wickets on the fourth day of the second Test in Rawalpindi to win the two-match series 1-0 — their first Test series win in two years.

Sri Lanka resumed on 309-9 and added just seven runs before Mohammad Abbas trapped Asitha Fernando lbw in his first over of the day, bowling them out for 316. Milan Rathnayake finished 66 not out, with Kusal Mendis (53) and Sonal Dinusha (54) also making half-centuries in the second innings.

Chasing 90, Pakistan lost debutant openers Tayyab Arif (13) and Mohammad Taha (4) early, but Aamer Jamal — promoted to No. 4 — smashed an unbeaten 34 off 22 balls and Azan Awais made 32 not out, the pair adding 53 for the third wicket. Jamal hit the winning boundary off Prabath Jayasuriya.

Pakistan had taken control on Day 1, bowling Sri Lanka out for 98 before making 325 in reply for a 227-run lead, with Ghazi Ghori top-scoring on 54. Abbas, who finished with 3-55 in the second innings, was named Player of the Match and Player of the Series. It was only Pakistan's third win from 11 matches in this World Test Championship cycle.

Sources: ESPNcricinfo, Reuters, ICC, AP, Cricbuzz.
