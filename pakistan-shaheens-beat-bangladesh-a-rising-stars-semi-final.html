Pakistan Shaheens beat Bangladesh A to reach Rising Stars final

Pakistan Shaheens chased 233 to beat Bangladesh A by five wickets in the Rising Stars semi-final in Doha, with Hasan Nawaz starring with bat and ball.

By Sheryar Khan • 28 Nov 2026

DOHA: Pakistan Shaheens reached the ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026 final on Friday, chasing down 233 to beat Bangladesh A by five wickets with 28 balls to spare at the West End Park International Cricket Stadium.

Bangladesh A chose to bat and posted 232 for 8, built almost entirely around Shahadat Hossain's unbeaten 103 off 118 balls. Ali Raza removed both openers inside five overs, and after Shahadat and Mahidul Islam Ankon had added 95 for the third wicket, Sufyan Moqim struck three times in two overs as Bangladesh A slipped from 110 for 2 to 118 for 5. Shahadat held the innings together to the end, reaching his century in the final over, while Moqim finished with 3 for 29 and Ali Raza and Hasan Nawaz took two wickets each.

The chase began badly, with Ripon Mondol dismissing both openers in the fourth over, but Nawaz and Shahzaib Khan settled it. The pair added 110 for the third wicket — Nawaz making 62 off 91 balls and Shahzaib, playing his first match of the tournament, 50 off 85.

Captain Abdul Samad's 44 off 33 balls and Farhan Yousaf's unbeaten 46 then finished the job, their 74-run stand taking Pakistan Shaheens to 233 for 5 in 45.2 overs. Nawaz was named Player of the Match for his 62 and two wickets.

The defending champions now face Sri Lanka A in Sunday's final in Doha. Bangladesh A's campaign is over.

Sources: ESPNcricinfo, Dawn, Geo Super.
