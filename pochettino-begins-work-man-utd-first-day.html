Pochettino begins work at Manchester United: 'No time to think about rest — we must be ready for Anfield'

Mauricio Pochettino arrived at Carrington on Wednesday, hours after ending his USA job with a 2-0 win over Haiti, and says United must be ready for Liverpool on Sunday.

By Sheryar Khan • 19 Nov 2026

Mauricio Pochettino has started work at Manchester United, arriving at the Carrington training ground on Wednesday, hours after ending his time as USA coach with a 2-0 win over Haiti in Cincinnati. After a seven-hour flight he went straight to Carrington, where he spent his first day with the technical and medical departments assessing the squad. His first full training session and team meeting come today as the internationals return.

"Very excited to be here. It's an honour, it's a privilege to be in this amazing club," he said. "I have plenty of energy... We need to be ready to compete on Sunday. Now it's not time to think about rest, it's not time to think about sleep."

United are 18th with one win from 10 league games; his first match is Sunday 22 Nov away at Liverpool at Anfield.
