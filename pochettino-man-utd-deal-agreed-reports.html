Glazer approves Pochettino appointment — Man Utd agree deal in principle, but wait goes on

Reports say Manchester United have agreed a deal in principle with Mauricio Pochettino, and co-owner Avram Glazer has approved the appointment — but Michael Carrick has not been officially sacked yet, and Pochettino will stay with the USA for the Haiti games. Full story.

By Sheryar Khan • 12 Nov 2026

REPORTS: This story is based on media reports. Manchester United have not confirmed any decision about Michael Carrick or Mauricio Pochettino.

Manchester United have moved a big step closer to appointing Mauricio Pochettino. Reports on Wednesday say the club reached a verbal agreement in principle with the USA coach, and co-owner Avram Glazer approved the appointment on Wednesday evening after a remote meeting. United have now formally contacted US Soccer about releasing Pochettino from his contract, which runs through the 2030 World Cup.

The money is not fully settled. A release clause of around £7.4m ($10m) is reported for Pochettino himself, and several outlets agree on that figure. But the total package — Pochettino plus his backroom staff — is reported differently: TEAMtalk puts it at £10.5m, while talkSPORT reports around $12m (£9m). Until the club confirms anything, the total cost remains a range, not a single number.

There will also be a wait. Pochettino is expected to stay with the USA for two CONCACAF Nations League games against Haiti — on Saturday in Miami and on Tuesday in Cincinnati — and he is due to speak to the media on Friday ahead of the first leg. United are reported to be comfortable with that delay, and one report says he could be in the dugout for the Liverpool match at Anfield on Sunday 22 November.

Michael Carrick, meanwhile, has not been officially sacked yet. Reports say he is expected to be formally informed on Thursday that he has lost his job, but as of Thursday morning he had not been told, and he was at Carrington on Wednesday working with the players not away on international duty. The final decision on Carrick rests with Sir Jim Ratcliffe, after chief executive Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox make their recommendation.

Pochettino is reported to be bringing four trusted members of his backroom team with him: assistants Jesus Perez and Miki D'Agostino, goalkeeping coach Toni Jimenez, and sports scientist Sebastiano Pochettino. The BBC also reports that Pochettino has been tasked with leading United into the Champions League next season — a big job for a side currently 18th in the Premier League after eight games without a win.

United have the rest of the international break to finalise the appointment before they return to action at Liverpool on 22 November. We will update this post if the club confirms anything.
