Pochettino starts Man Utd job on Wednesday — first game Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday

Mauricio Pochettino takes charge at Carrington on Wednesday 18 November, a day after his final game as USA coach against Haiti, with Liverpool waiting at Anfield on Sunday.

By Sheryar Khan • 17 Nov 2026

Mauricio Pochettino will start work as Manchester United head coach on Wednesday 18 November, a day after his final match in charge of the United States.

Pochettino leads the USA for the last time on Tuesday night in the second leg of their CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final against Haiti in Cincinnati. The Americans won the first leg 6-0 in Miami on Saturday, so the tie is all but decided.

United confirmed Pochettino as their new head coach on Friday 13 November. He has signed a two-and-a-half-year contract until June 2029, with United paying a release clause of around £7.4m–£7.5m ($10m) to US Soccer. He had only signed a contract extension with US Soccer through the 2030 World Cup three months ago.

A big first week

His first match is a big one: Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday 22 November, at 4:30pm. United are 18th in the Premier League with eight points from 10 games, and three days later they face Sporting Lisbon in the Champions League.

Three of Pochettino's coaches are already in England. Senior assistant Jesus Perez, goalkeeping coach Toni Jimenez and fitness coach Sebastiano Pochettino — his son — flew to Manchester after the 6-0 win over Haiti. Assistant coach Miguel D'Agostino, fitness coach Sylvia Tuya Vinas and analyst Alex Scott will follow after Tuesday's game.

"A much better coach"

Pochettino said his two years with the national team made him a better coach. "I think I am a much better coach now," he said, adding that he missed the daily work of club football and wants to prove himself again in the Premier League.

He also had a message for Pep Guardiola, who is reported to be the USA's top target to replace him. "Take it," Pochettino said. "Why? Because it's a perfect moment to arrive at the federation. Everything is on the right path, in the right direction."

USA midfielder Weston McKennie, who plays for Juventus, has left the door open to joining United. Pochettino said it was not the right time to discuss transfers, but added that United are among the very top clubs and a dream for many players.

Pochettino becomes United's eighth permanent manager since Sir Alex Ferguson retired in 2013. He leaves the USA job with 21 wins from 35 matches.
