Pochettino's USA farewell ends in win over Haiti as he starts Man Utd job today

Mauricio Pochettino signed off as USMNT head coach with a 2-0 win over Haiti in Cincinnati, and takes over at Manchester United today.

By Sheryar Khan • 18 Nov 2026

CINCINNATI: Mauricio Pochettino's time as United States head coach ended with a win, as the USMNT beat Haiti 2-0 in the second leg of their CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final on Tuesday — 8-0 on aggregate.

Christian Pulisic opened the scoring in the 25th minute with a stunning overhead kick from Weston McKennie's cross, the ball bouncing in off the far post. Eighteen-year-old Julian Hall made it 2-0 just before half-time, finishing a chipped McKennie pass for his fifth goal in six appearances for the United States.

The win sends the USA into the Nations League Finals at SoFi Stadium in March 2027. Pochettino, who took the US job in September 2024 and led them to the 2026 World Cup round of 16, said goodbye to the fans in a video message: "Thank you so much from the bottom of my heart... Always in my heart, always one of you."

He now flies to England to start work as Manchester United's new head coach today, Wednesday 18 November. His first match is away at Liverpool on Sunday 22 November, with United 18th in the Premier League.

Sources: NBC Sports, USA Today, NY Post, Fox Sports.
