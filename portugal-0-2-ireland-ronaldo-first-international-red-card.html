Portugal 0-2 Ireland: Ronaldo sent off for first time as Portugal's World Cup hopes hang in balance

Cristiano Ronaldo received his first-ever international red card as Portugal lost 2-0 to Ireland in Dublin — Troy Parrott scored twice, and Ronaldo now misses the Armenia decider with a World Cup ban still possible.

By Sheryar Khan • 14 Nov 2026

Cristiano Ronaldo was sent off for the first time in his international career as Portugal suffered a shock 2-0 defeat to the Republic of Ireland in Dublin on Friday, 13 Nov, leaving their World Cup qualification to a final-day decider. Troy Parrott scored both goals for Ireland, in the 17th and 45th minutes, as the hosts stunned the European champions at the Aviva Stadium. Ronaldo's night got worse on 61 minutes: after an off-the-ball elbow on Ireland defender Dara O'Shea, the referee first showed a yellow card, then upgraded it to a straight red after a VAR review. It was the first red card of Ronaldo's Portugal career in his 226th appearance — a record in men's international football. The 40-year-old sarcastically applauded the home crowd as he walked off, and he has since been released from the Portugal camp, according to Portuguese outlet A Bola. Ronaldo will definitely miss Sunday's final qualifier against Armenia in Porto (15 Nov), when a win or a draw will seal Portugal's direct qualification for the 2026 World Cup. The bigger question is still open: FIFA's disciplinary committee has not yet decided the length of the ban, and a straight red for violent conduct can carry a longer suspension — so Ronaldo could still miss the start of the World Cup itself. That is a risk, not a confirmed ban. Portugal coach Roberto Martinez called the defeat "a game to forget", and Bruno Fernandes — who missed the Ireland match through suspension — returns for the Armenia game.

Sources: All Football, AZERTAC, WION.
