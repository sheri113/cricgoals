Portugal vs Armenia: Fernandes and Neves hit hat-tricks as Portugal seal World Cup place with 9-1 win

Portugal thrashed Armenia 9-1 in Porto without suspended Cristiano Ronaldo, as Bruno Fernandes and João Neves both scored hat-tricks to seal a place at the 2026 World Cup.

By Sheryar Khan • 16 Nov 2026

PORTO: Portugal thrashed Armenia 9-1 at the Estádio do Dragão on Sunday night to seal their place at the 2026 World Cup — and they did it without Cristiano Ronaldo.

No Ronaldo, no problem

Ronaldo was suspended after his first international red card, for elbowing Ireland's Dara O'Shea in the 61st minute of Thursday's 2-0 defeat in Dublin, and Portugal had to win to guarantee direct qualification. They needed less than seven minutes to settle any nerves: Bruno Fernandes's free-kick was pushed onto the post by goalkeeper Henri Avagyan, and Renato Veiga headed in the rebound. Armenia briefly stunned the home crowd when captain Eduard Spertsyan equalised in the 18th minute, but Portugal then scored four more before half-time. Gonçalo Ramos intercepted a back pass and rounded Avagyan in the 28th minute, João Neves scored from the edge of the box two minutes later and then curled a free-kick into the top corner, and Fernandes converted a penalty in first-half stoppage time after Rúben Dias was fouled. Portugal led 5-1 at the break.

Two hat-tricks in one night

Fernandes struck again six minutes after the restart, fed by Ramos, and completed his hat-trick from the penalty spot in the 72nd minute after debutant substitute Carlos Forbs was felled. It was his first hat-trick for Portugal — and his first goals in 12 months. His third goal was his 28th for Portugal, taking him past Rui Costa and Hélder Postiga into sixth place on the country's all-time scorers list. Neves followed suit in the 81st minute, smashing home after Veiga headed a Fernandes cross into his path, to complete a hat-trick of his own — his first three goals for Portugal. Substitute Francisco Conceição added the ninth in stoppage time, finishing a pass from João Félix. Portugal had 34 shots to Armenia's four.

Qualified at the third attempt

The win sealed top spot in Group F with 13 points, three ahead of Ireland, who beat Hungary 3-2 in Budapest and go to the play-offs. Portugal will play at a ninth World Cup, their seventh in a row, having sealed qualification at the third attempt after the draw in Hungary in October and the shock defeat in Dublin. Manager Roberto Martínez praised his players for stepping up and showing leadership in Ronaldo's absence. Ronaldo celebrated on social media, writing: "WE ARE IN THE WORLD CUP! LET'S GO ALL OUT, PORTUGAL!" But FIFA has not yet decided whether his ban will be extended beyond the one match he served against Armenia, so he could still miss the start of the tournament itself.
