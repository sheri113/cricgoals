Portugal vs Armenia preview: Portugal must win without suspended Ronaldo to seal World Cup place

Portugal host Armenia at the Estádio do Dragão on Sunday night knowing a win seals their place at the 2026 World Cup — but they must do it without Cristiano Ronaldo, suspended after his first international red card.

By Sheryar Khan • 15 Nov 2026

PORTO: Portugal host Armenia at the Estádio do Dragão on Sunday night (7:00 PM PKT) in a match they must win to guarantee their place at the 2026 World Cup — and they must do it without Cristiano Ronaldo.

No Ronaldo — Ramos leads the line

Ronaldo is suspended after his first international red card, for elbowing Ireland's Dara O'Shea in the 61st minute of Friday's 2-0 defeat in Dublin — a result that left Portugal's qualification hanging in the balance. FIFA has not yet decided whether the ban will be extended beyond tonight, so Ronaldo could miss the start of the World Cup itself. In his place, Paris Saint-Germain striker Gonçalo Ramos is expected to lead the attack. Ramos averages almost a goal every two games for Portugal, and manager Roberto Martínez has backed him to step up. Bruno Fernandes returns from suspension and is Portugal's top scorer among the players available tonight — a goal against Armenia would take him level with Rui Costa on 26 goals for Portugal. Pedro Neto is out with a groin injury, and Rafael Leão is expected to start on the left ahead of João Félix.

What Portugal need

Portugal top Group F with 10 points, ahead of Hungary on eight, Ireland on seven and Armenia on three. A win tonight seals direct qualification for the World Cup. A draw would leave Portugal waiting on the result of Hungary vs Ireland in Budapest, played at the same time. Armenia, already eliminated, have three points from six games. Portugal won the reverse fixture 5-0 in Armenia in September.
