Introduction

Welcome to Cricgoals. Cricgoals ("we", "our" or "us") operates this website, which provides cricket and football news and match analysis to readers around the world. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use that information, and the choices available to you. By accessing or using this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We collect a limited amount of information, in the following ways:

Information you provide to us. When you contact us through the contact form on this website, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, your email address and the contents of your message.

Information collected automatically. Like most website operators, we collect information that your browser sends whenever you visit our website ("Log Data"). This may include your computer's Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, the pages of our website that you visit, the date and time of your visit, the time spent on those pages, and other diagnostic data. This information is not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

Cookies and similar technologies. We and our partners use cookies and similar tracking technologies, as described in the section on Cookies and Tracking Technologies below.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • to operate, maintain and improve this website and its content;
  • to understand how readers use the website, so that we can publish the coverage our audience values most;
  • to remember your preferences, such as the language you have selected;
  • to respond to messages and enquiries you send us through the contact form;
  • to display advertising, including advertising served by Google AdSense;
  • to monitor the security of the website and to prevent fraud and abuse; and
  • to comply with any applicable legal obligations.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your device when you visit it. We use cookies to remember your preferences (for example, your chosen language), to understand in aggregate how visitors use our website, and to serve advertising. Third parties, including Google, also place cookies through our website for analytics and advertising purposes.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies, or to alert you when a cookie is being sent, by adjusting your browser settings. If you do not accept cookies, some parts of this website may not function as smoothly, although our content will remain available to you.

Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense, a service provided by Google, to display advertisements on this website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our website. Google's use of advertising cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, enables Google and its partners to serve ads to our visitors based on their visits to this website and/or to other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings page. You may also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising through the Network Advertising Initiative's opt-out page. Please note that we do not control the cookies used by third-party advertisers, and their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services to help us understand, in aggregate and anonymised form, how visitors use this website — for example, how many people visit, which articles are read most often, and the countries from which our readers visit. These services use cookies and similar technologies to collect such information, and they do not provide us with information that identifies individual visitors.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the Internet, and no method of electronic storage, is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below and we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

Our articles may contain links to external websites that are not operated by us. If you follow a link to a third-party site, you will be directed to that third party's website. This Privacy Policy applies only to Cricgoals; we have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content or privacy practices of any third-party sites, and we strongly advise you to review the privacy policy of every site you visit.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights in relation to your personal information, including the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, the right to ask us to correct or delete it, and the right to object to, or request a restriction of, certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below; we will respond to legitimate requests within a reasonable time.
