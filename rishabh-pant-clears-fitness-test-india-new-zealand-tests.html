Rishabh Pant Passes Fitness Test Ahead of India vs New Zealand Tests

India receive a major boost as wicketkeeper-batter Rishabh Pant clears his fitness assessment and prepares to join the squad in New Zealand ahead of the two-Test series starting November 19.

By Sheryar Khan • 12 Nov 2026

India have received a significant boost ahead of their upcoming Test series against New Zealand, with wicketkeeper-batter Rishabh Pant successfully clearing his fitness test. Pant is travelling to New Zealand to join the Indian squad after completing his rehabilitation and receiving fitness clearance from the BCCI Centre of Excellence on Thursday, November 12.

Pant Completes Fitness Assessments

According to reports, Pant completed two fitness and match-simulation assessments on November 10 and 11 before receiving approval. The left-handed wicketkeeper-batter had been recovering from a side strain that kept him away from international action. His place in India's Test squad had been subject to fitness clearance. His successful assessment removes that uncertainty and allows him to rejoin the national team ahead of an important overseas assignment.

India Receive a Boost Before the Test Series

Pant's return gives India another experienced wicketkeeping option alongside Dhruv Jurel. His batting ability and experience in challenging overseas conditions could prove valuable for India's plans in New Zealand. However, his final selection in the playing XI will depend on the team's requirements and match conditions.

India vs New Zealand Test Schedule

India are scheduled to play two Tests against New Zealand as part of the ICC World Test Championship 2025–27. The first Test will be played in Wellington from November 19 to 23, while the second Test will be played in Christchurch from November 27 to December 1. India will be looking to make the most of their squad depth as they prepare for the two-match series.

What Pant's Return Means for India

Pant's fitness clearance is encouraging news for India ahead of the opening Test. His ability to contribute with both bat and gloves gives the team additional flexibility. The next step will be to assess his match readiness after joining the squad. India's final playing combination will become clearer closer to the first Test.
