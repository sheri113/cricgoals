Pakistan Shaheens hold off Nepal to top Group B as Sooryavanshi's record 204 fires India A into the semi-finals

Nepal bowled Pakistan Shaheens out for 168 in Doha, while 15-year-old Vaibhav Sooryavanshi smashed a record 204 off 53 balls as India A chased 250 in 14.2 overs.

By Sheryar Khan • 24 Nov 2026

DOHA: Pakistan Shaheens were bowled out for 168 by Nepal in their final Group B match of the ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026 in Doha on Tuesday, 24 November — and in the day's other Group B match, 15-year-old Vaibhav Sooryavanshi smashed a record 204 off 53 balls as India A beat UAE by seven wickets.

Rashid Khan took five wickets for 39 runs as Nepal bowled the Shaheens out in 42 overs after Pakistan chose to bat. Only Rohail Nazir, unbeaten on 64 off 83 balls, held the innings together after a collapse that saw Pakistan fall from 65 for 4 to 88 for 7. Nazir and Ahmed Daniyal added 70 for the eighth wicket, but Pakistan did not use 48 of their 300 balls.

Chasing 169, Nepal were bowled out for just 72 in 17.3 overs. Mayan Yadav top-scored with 41 off 50 balls, and Basir Ahamad's 11 was the only other double-figure score. Sufyan Moqim took 3 for 17 and Mohammad Hasnain 3 for 21, with Mohammad Imran taking 2 for 29, as Pakistan won by 96 runs. Rohail Nazir was named Player of the Match.

Earlier in the day, Sooryavanshi produced one of the most destructive innings in List A history. Chasing 250 against UAE, the 15-year-old reached his century in 28 balls and his double-century in 52 — both the fastest ever in List A cricket — hitting 28 sixes and seven fours at a strike rate of 384.90. India A reached 254 for 3 in just 14.2 overs, with 214 balls to spare. Rinku Singh finished unbeaten on 27.

UAE had posted 249 for 8, led by Harpreet Singh Bhatia's 130 off 144 balls, with Arshad Khan taking 4 for 43 for India A. Sooryavanshi was dismissed with only five runs needed, caught at long-off trying to finish the game with another six.

The win means Pakistan Shaheens finish top of Group B with six points from three wins, and will face Bangladesh A in the second semi-final on Friday, 27 November, at 3:00 pm PKT. India A finish second with four points and face Sri Lanka A in the first semi-final on Thursday, 26 November, at 3:00 pm PKT. The final is on Sunday, 29 November.
