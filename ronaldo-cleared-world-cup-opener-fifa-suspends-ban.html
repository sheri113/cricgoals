Cristiano Ronaldo cleared to play Portugal's World Cup opener as FIFA suspends two-match ban

FIFA has banned Cristiano Ronaldo for three matches over his red card against Ireland — but two are suspended for a year, and the one he served against Armenia counts, so Portugal's captain will be available for the World Cup opener.

By Sheryar Khan • 16 Nov 2026

LISBON: Cristiano Ronaldo will be available for Portugal's World Cup opener after FIFA suspended two matches of his three-match ban for a year.

Ronaldo was sent off for the first time in 226 international appearances in Thursday's 2-0 defeat in Dublin, elbowing Ireland defender Dara O'Shea in the 61st minute. Violent conduct normally carries a three-match ban — enough to rule him out of the start of the tournament. But FIFA's Disciplinary Committee suspended the serving of the remaining two matches under a one-year probation period, in line with Article 27 of the FIFA Disciplinary Code.

The one match Ronaldo has already served — Sunday's 9-1 win over Armenia, which sealed Portugal's qualification — counts as the ban. He scored five goals in qualifying and will now play in his sixth World Cup. Portugal find out their group opponents at the draw on 5 December.

There is a condition: if Ronaldo commits another offence of a similar nature and gravity during the probation period, the two suspended matches will apply immediately at Portugal's next official matches — which would be the opening World Cup games. The decision can be appealed to the FIFA Appeal Committee.

Sources: AFP, ESPN, The Athletic.
