Scotland vs Denmark 4-2: Scotland qualify for the World Cup after 28 years

Scotland beat Denmark 4-2 at Hampden Park to qualify for the World Cup for the first time since 1998 — McTominay's overhead kick, Tierney and McLean's stoppage-time goals seal a famous night.

By Sheryar Khan • 14 Nov 2026

Scotland are going to the World Cup for the first time in 28 years. They beat Denmark 4-2 in a winner-takes-all qualifier at Hampden Park on Friday, 13 Nov, with two goals deep in stoppage time sealing a famous night in Glasgow. Scott McTominay opened the scoring in the third minute with a spectacular overhead kick, meeting a cross and beating Kasper Schmeichel.

Denmark fight back twice

Denmark levelled through a Rasmus Højlund penalty on 57 minutes, after Andy Robertson fouled Gustav Isaksen, and even after Rasmus Kristensen was sent off for a second yellow card four minutes later, the visitors kept coming. Lawrence Shankland restored Scotland's lead on 78 minutes, turning in Lewis Ferguson's corner from close range, but Patrick Dorgu equalised again three minutes later, and a draw would have sent Denmark to the finals instead.

Two stoppage-time goals seal it

Then the substitutes finished it: Kieran Tierney curled a left-footed shot from outside the box inside the far post three minutes into stoppage time for 3-2, and five minutes later Kenny McLean won the ball near the halfway line, saw Schmeichel stranded upfield and lobbed him from his own half for 4-2. Scotland topped Group C and qualified automatically, ending a 28-year wait since France 1998. Manager Steve Clarke became the first manager to lead Scotland to three major tournaments.

Spain and Belgium also qualify

Spain and Belgium also qualified on the same night — Spain drew 2-2 with Turkey, Belgium beat Liechtenstein 7-0 — taking the confirmed field to 42 of the 48 teams for the finals, which run from 11 June to 19 July 2026 across the United States, Canada and Mexico.

Sources: All Football, TML News, Scottish Government, Daily Record.
