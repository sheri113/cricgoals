Sri Lanka A beat India A to reach Rising Stars final

Sri Lanka A chased 318 to beat India A by eight wickets in the Rising Stars semi-final in Doha, with centuries from Lasith Croospulle and Sineth Jayawardena.

By Sheryar Khan • 27 Nov 2026

DOHA: Sri Lanka A stormed into the ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026 final on Thursday, chasing down 318 to beat India A by eight wickets with 51 balls to spare at the West End Park International Cricket Stadium.

India A chose to bat and posted 317 for 8. Abhishek Sharma led the way with 78 off 63 balls after adding 80 with Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, who made 40 off 27, and captain Tilak Varma added 53. But Sri Lanka A struck through the middle overs — India A slipped from 197 for 2 to 235 for 6 — before Shahbaz Ahmed's unbeaten 54 off 48 balls, in a 73-run seventh-wicket stand with Tanush Kotian, lifted them past 300. Janith Liyanage, Nipun Dhananjaya and Ravindu Fernando took two wickets each.

The chase was settled by the openers. Sineth Jayawardena smashed 105 off 70 balls, with ten fours and six sixes, and Lasith Croospulle made 116 off 107 as the pair added 174 for the first wicket. Sri Lanka A were 85 without loss after ten overs, and India A were left to rue two early chances — Croospulle's leading edge off the first ball fell short of Tilak Varma at mid-off, and Jayawardena's shot burst through Tanush Kotian's hands in the second over.

Jayawardena, named Player of the Match, fell with the score on 174, but there was no way back for India A. Nishan Madushka finished unbeaten on 58 and captain Pavan Rathnayake struck 31 not out off 16 balls as Sri Lanka A reached 318 for 2 in 41.3 overs.

Unbeaten Sri Lanka A now wait for Sunday's final in Doha, where they will face the winner of Friday's second semi-final between Pakistan Shaheens and Bangladesh A. India A's campaign is over.

Sources: ESPNcricinfo, ANI, PTI.
